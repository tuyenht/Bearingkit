# Field defects

Defects in delivered work, one line each, added to and never rewritten. Started 2026-11-02 on the owner's label for open choice 6 of `docs/specs/2026-11-01-real-defect-pilot-design.md` (`docs/autopilot/decisions.md` entry 89). That spec, section on the defect record, says what puts a line here and when; rules about how sessions work stay `RULE` lines in the handoffs.

Format:

```
D-<n> | <date> | <where: this repository, or another project in one anonymized sentence> | <what went wrong> | <how it was found, and what missed it> | <anchor: commit, log entry, or "owner's word"> | <candidate | point <id> | not a point: reason>
```

At each close the session lists its `fix` commits and gives each a line here or a stated reason it is not a defect in delivered work; with none, the handoff says "Field defects: none". The production SaaS enters only through the owner's own anonymized sentence.
