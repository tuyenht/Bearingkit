## Design

**1. `references/vertical-slices.md`**, new, about 35 lines, opened when the plan has more than one phase or changes something many call sites use.
- A phase is a **vertical slice**: a narrow but complete path through every layer the feature touches (data, logic, interface, tests), **demoable or verifiable on its own**; "all the schema", "all the endpoints", "the tests" are layers, not phases. The first slice is the thinnest end-to-end path (the tracer bullet).
- **Prefactoring comes first** and is its own phase: "make the change easy, then make the easy change".
- Each phase names its **blocking edges**: the phases that must be done before it can start, or "none"; work proceeds on the frontier (phases whose blockers are done).
- A phase is **sized to fit one fresh session** (the kit's phases may be executed by a subagent each, `bk-build/references/executing.md`).
- **Wide refactors are the exception.** One mechanical change whose blast radius fans across the code (a renamed column, a retyped shared symbol) is sequenced **expand, migrate, contract**: add the new form beside the old; move the callers in batches sized by blast radius, each batch a phase blocked by the expand; remove the old form in a last phase blocked by every batch, once no caller remains. **A kit addition, not in the source**: the blast radius includes readers outside the repository (another team's job, a client, a stored export); a search of the code cannot show that such a reader has moved, so the contract phase names that reader and what confirms it has moved. This sentence is the rule hazard P3 below measures, and is named as such under "Limits". When the batches cannot stay green alone they share an integration branch and block a final integrate-and-verify phase.
- From the idea rows, two lines, the designer's choice under point 2 (a row is taken only if it changes a step in a way a written plan can show): **split signals** (an "and" in a phase's title, many exit criteria, two subsystems: row 449); **the plan is re-read after the first slice lands**, and revised where the slice showed it wrong (row 216).
- **Not carried**: publishing to a tracker, triage labels, `.scratch/` ticket files, the parent-issue rules; "avoid file paths and code" with its prototype-snippet exception (`:105`; the kit's plan is executed at once by `bk-build`, not parked in a tracker: `writing-plans.md:31` stays); "iterate until the user approves" (the gate decides); titles in the project's glossary vocabulary and respect for its decision records (`:21`; that is `bk-spec`'s ground, and its reference for it is on `p5a-bk-spec`, unmerged). Of the two idea rows taken, the halves left out: "riskiest task first" of row 449 (the first slice is the thinnest path, and the re-read after it is where a wrong assumption surfaces) and "entry criteria as checkable preconditions" of row 216 (the blocking edges are the precondition a plan can show).
