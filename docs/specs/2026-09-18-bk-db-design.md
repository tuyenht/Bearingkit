## What it is for

One person doing what a team gives its DBA: finding why a statement, a table or the whole database is slow from the database's own evidence, and changing indexes and tables that hold live data without an outage and with a way back. The scope test of spec §1 holds: each step is one a DBA owns.

## Two paths, one gate

- **Diagnose**, read-only: name the database and whether the connection reaches production; check that the database is the slow part at all; classify the symptom; measure (statement statistics, then the plan); name the cause with its evidence.
- **Change**, only after a diagnosis: the baseline first, one change per measurement, the online form with its lock named, the way back written before, proposed as COUNCIL, applied through a migration, measured the same way, kept only if the numbers moved.

The protocol already classes schema and migrations as COUNCIL. `bk-db` adds no gate; it makes the proposal concrete (baseline, lock, expected effect with its method, write cost, way back) and keeps diagnosis read-only, with ending a session, `VACUUM FULL`, a statistics refresh and a setting change on a shared or production database named as changes.
