## Decisions taken in this sprint (the owner's standing delegation, "Tiếp tục theo khuyến nghị tốt nhất", resume prompt of 2026-09-23)

- **A security finding carries the same anchors as any other**: an added line, the source, the sink and the path, scored with the one rubric. The source's investigation aims at recall and its refute pass at precision; the kit keeps both passes and puts its own 80-point threshold after them. So `review-lenses.md:37` (abstract "security" is not a finding) and the lens agree, and the lens's own "not a finding" list says the same thing.
- **Smells go through the rubric**, not into a separate list of judgement calls as in the source: the kit has one threshold, and a second report tier would be the noise the rubric exists to remove. The source's two rules are kept word for word in meaning (the project's standard wins; a smell is named as possible, never a violation).
- **The security lens runs on a hot path without the flag**, and on a diff that adds or changes an entry point or a sink. v1 L4 already asked for `--security` whenever a hot path is flagged; the second trigger goes further than v1 because the source's method starts from entry points and sinks, so a diff that touches neither leaves the lens nothing to trace, and one that does is where its findings live. The flag stays as the way to force it elsewhere.
- **"Owner's earlier `review`"** (a v1 §7.1 source) is not in this repository: no file names a path for it, and `docs/plans/2026-09-10-owner-migration.md:14` only says project-level copies of the owner's earlier commands exist on this machine. Finding them means reading other repositories (COUNCIL), so the `Sources:` line names what the skill uses. The session recommended dropping that source unless the owner points at it, and the owner approved the session's recommendations after the commit ("Audit kỹ các đề xuất khuyến nghị xử lý ở trên đã chuẩn chưa?
