# bk-design · A default look is either absent or justified out loud

**Prompt** (en)
> Make it look modern and clean.

**Setup**
A brief that pins nothing: no palette, no typeface, no direction.

**Expected**
1. The generic-default clusters in references/distinctive-ui.md are checked against the proposal.
2. Where the design lands on one of them, the brief is quoted as asking for it; where it does not, the choice is tied to the subject.
3. The free axes are spent on choices that come from the subject's own world, not on the default palette and the default card grid.

**Fails if**
- A cream background with a terracotta accent, or identical rounded cards with one shadow, appears with no reason given.
- An all-caps eyebrow label is added above every heading.
