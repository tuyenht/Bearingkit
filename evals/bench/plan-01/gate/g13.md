# CSAT rating: implementation notes

Implement the approved spec in `docs/specs/csat-rating.md`. Customers get a way to give a resolved ticket a score from 1 to 5 from the portal, once per ticket and only for tickets of their own organisation; the assigned agent gets an email when the score is 1 or 2; a nightly job writes a weekly summary per agent; and `assignee_id` is renamed `owner_id` everywhere. Afterwards add tests for the above and make sure the suite passes.
