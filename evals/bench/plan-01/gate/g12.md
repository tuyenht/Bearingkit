# Plan for the customer satisfaction rating (spec: `docs/specs/csat-rating.md`)

Nothing here is built yet. Two questions first, with the answer the tasks below work from.

**Q1. `exports/tickets.json` is read by finance's reporting job, which finance deploys when it suits them. If the rename reaches that file, their job breaks on a day we do not choose. What do we do with the export?** Recommended, and what this plan does: the export keeps writing `assignee_id` and gains `owner_id` beside it; the old key is dropped only once finance confirms their job reads the new one (task 3).

**Q2. A score of 1 or 2 on a ticket nobody owns: who gets the email?** Recommended: nobody; the rating is stored.
