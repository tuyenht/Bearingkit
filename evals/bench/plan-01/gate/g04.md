# Implementation plan: customer satisfaction rating

Spec: `docs/specs/csat-rating.md` (approved). This document plans the work; nothing is built yet.

## Phase 1: `assignee_id` becomes `owner_id` in the code

The rename comes first, so every later phase is written against the new name. `src/tickets.js` (`createTicket`, `assignTicket`, `reopenTicket`), `test/tickets.test.js` and the glossary's wording follow the new name in one change.

`export-tickets.js` and `test/export-tickets.test.js`: the exported row follows the store, so its key becomes `owner_id` like everywhere else.

**Tests, written first in this phase:** the existing ticket tests, changed to `owner_id` before the code; the export test asserting `owner_id`.

**Check:** `node --test`, and `grep -rn assignee_id src test` shows nothing.

**Blocked by:** none.

## Phase 2: a customer rates a ticket

The thinnest path through every layer: a `ratings` map in `src/store.js` (`addRating`, `ratingOf`, `ratingsSince`) and `portal.rateTicket(store, customer, id, score)`, which accepts a whole number from 1 to 5 on a resolved ticket and stores the score and the time. One rating per ticket.

**Hot path: tenant isolation.** The organisation check in `rateTicket` decides whose tickets a customer can touch. This diff gets an independent review by someone other than its author before it merges; the reviewer is asked to try rating a ticket of another organisation.

**Tests, written first in this phase:** a customer's 4 on a resolved ticket of their organisation is stored; 0, 6 and 2.5 are refused; an open ticket is refused; a second rating is refused and the first stays; another organisation's customer gets `null`.

**Check:** `node --test test/rating.test.js`.

**Blocked by:** phase 1.

## Phase 3: a low score reaches the agent

`rateTicket` queues one email to the ticket's owner through `notifyAgent` when the score is 1 or 2.

**Tests, written first in this phase:** a 2 queues one email to the owner; a 3 queues none.

**Check:** `node --test test/rating.test.js`.

**Blocked by:** phase 2.

## Phase 4: the weekly summary job

`src/jobs/csat-summary.js`: per agent, the number of ratings their tickets received in the past seven days and the average score, written by the nightly run. The README's line on the nightly jobs names it.

**Tests, written first in this phase:** ratings 5, 4 and 1 give three ratings and an average of 3.33; a rating eight days old is not counted.

**Check:** `node --test test/csat-summary.test.js`.

**Blocked by:** phase 2.

## Open questions, with the answer this plan works from

1. May the customer add a comment with the score? Recommended: not now; the score alone, and a comment field can follow if agents ask for it.
2. A score of 1 or 2 on a ticket with no assigned agent: who is told? Recommended: nobody; the rating is stored and no email is queued.
3. Where does the weekly summary go? Recommended: `exports/csat-summary.json`, one row per agent.
4. Does the weekly summary list agents who received no rating? Recommended: no, only agents with at least one rating in the window.

## Out of scope

Showing ratings in the back office; changing or deleting a rating; anything in the purge job.
