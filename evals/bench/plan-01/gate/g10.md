# Implementation plan: customer satisfaction rating

Spec: `docs/specs/csat-rating.md` (approved). Nothing is built by this document.

## Step 1: data model

`src/store.js` gains a `ratings` map with `addRating`, `ratingOf` and `ratingsSince`, keyed by ticket, holding the score and the time. The ticket row's `assignee_id` becomes `owner_id`.

## Step 2: rename `assignee_id` to `owner_id`

`src/tickets.js`, `test/tickets.test.js` and the glossary follow the new name.

**The export is the exception.** `exports/tickets.json` is read by the finance team's reporting job, which they deploy on their own schedule: a rename there breaks a reader we can neither see nor test. So `export-tickets.js` writes `owner_id` and keeps writing `assignee_id` beside it, and its test asserts both. Removing `assignee_id` from the export is not part of this plan: it happens only after finance confirms that their job reads `owner_id` (asking finance is part of this step).

## Step 3: `rateTicket` in `src/portal.js`

`portal.rateTicket(store, customer, id, score)`: a whole number from 1 to 5, a resolved ticket only, one rating per ticket.

**Hot path: tenant isolation.** The organisation check in `rateTicket` decides whose tickets a customer can touch. This diff gets an independent review by someone other than its author before it merges; the reviewer is asked to try rating a ticket of another organisation.

## Step 4: low-score email

`rateTicket` queues one email to the ticket's owner through `notifyAgent` when the score is 1 or 2.

## Step 5: nightly job

`src/jobs/csat-summary.js`: per agent, the number of ratings of the past seven days and the average score. The README's line on the nightly jobs names it.

## Step 6: tests

- Rename: the ticket tests on `owner_id`; the export test asserting both keys.
- Rating: a customer's 4 is stored; 0, 6 and 2.5 refused; an open ticket refused; a second rating refused; another organisation's customer gets `null`.
- Email: a 2 queues one email, a 3 none.
- Job: 5, 4 and 1 give three ratings and 3.33; a rating eight days old is not counted.

Run `node --test` at the end.

## Build order

1. The rename, with its tests.
2. The store's ratings and `rateTicket`, with the rating tests.
3. The low-score email, with its tests.
4. The nightly job, with its tests.

## Open questions, with the answer this plan works from

1. May the customer add a comment with the score? Recommended: not now; the score alone, and a comment field can follow if agents ask for it.
2. A score of 1 or 2 on a ticket with no assigned agent: who is told? Recommended: nobody; the rating is stored and no email is queued.
3. Where does the weekly summary go? Recommended: `exports/csat-summary.json`, one row per agent.
4. Does the weekly summary list agents who received no rating? Recommended: no, only agents with at least one rating in the window.

## Out of scope

Showing ratings in the back office; changing or deleting a rating.
