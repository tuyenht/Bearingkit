# Reopen a ticket from the portal

## Request, restated

In your words: customers reopen a closed ticket within 14 days and the assigned agent is notified. Technical reading: a portal action that sets the status back to open and queues a notification. The two readings agree.

## Terms

- **Resolved**: the agent is done; the customer may still answer.
- **Closed**: final. A closed ticket is never changed again.
- **Assignee**: the agent who owns the ticket.

## Touchpoints

- `src/portal.js`: add `reopen(store, customer, id)` next to `reply`.
- `src/notify.js`: queue the notification.
- `src/jobs/purge-closed.js` and `src/jobs/close-resolved.js`: nightly jobs, not touched by this work.

## Edge cases

1. The ticket is already open: nothing happens.
2. The customer presses the button twice: the second press is ignored.
3. The window has passed: the button is hidden.

## Assumptions

- The SLA due time is set at creation (high confidence).
- Notifications are sent by email (high confidence).

## Open questions

1. Should the portal reuse the status names customers already see? Recommended: yes.
2. Should the SLA behaviour on reopen be decided now or later? Recommended: later.
3. Should the button text be "Reopen" or "Open again"? Recommended: "Reopen".
4. Should there be a confirmation dialog? Recommended: yes.
5. Should the reopen be logged? Recommended: yes.
