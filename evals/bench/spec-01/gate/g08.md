# Reopen a ticket from the portal

## Request, restated

In your words: customers reopen a closed ticket within 14 days and the assigned agent is notified. Technical reading: a portal action that moves a ticket back to open and queues an email. The two differ on the word "closed" (see Terms).

## Terms

- **Closed** in this project means final; a closed ticket is never changed again. Your request uses "closed" for a ticket the customer wants back, which the project calls **resolved**. This spec uses the project's words and treats the request as being about resolved tickets.

## Already there

- Agents reopen through `reopenTicket` (`src/tickets.js`); a customer's reply to a resolved ticket reopens it too (`src/portal.js`). The new action calls `reopenTicket`.

## Edge cases

1. The 14-day window: a ticket is closed three days after it is resolved and its messages are purged seven days after that, so 14 days cannot be honoured for closed tickets. For resolved tickets the window is the three days until closing.
2. No assignee on the ticket: no agent is notified; the ticket returns to the unassigned queue.
3. A ticket of a different organisation: the action answers "not found", as the other portal functions do; this is tenant scoping and gets an independent review.
4. Reopen pressed twice: the second call finds the ticket open and changes nothing.

## Open questions

1. Is "resolved" what you meant by "closed"? Recommended: yes.
2. May a ticket be reopened any number of times? Recommended: yes, no limit.
3. Should the customer state a reason? Recommended: optional.
4. Should the customer see who the assignee is after reopening? Recommended: no.

## Recorded assumptions (assumed, not confirmed)

- The SLA due time restarts at reopening; default: eight hours from the reopen.
- The reopen button also shows on mobile; default: yes.

## Out of scope

- Changing the purge or the close job.
- Reopening by email reply.

## Acceptance criteria

- A resolved ticket of the customer's organisation becomes open and its assignee has one queued email.
- A ticket of another organisation: "not found", nothing changes.
