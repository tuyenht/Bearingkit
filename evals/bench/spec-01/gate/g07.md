# Reopen from portal: spec

| Area | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Window | Requested: 14 days after closing. `src/jobs/purge-closed.js` deletes a closed ticket's messages after 7 days. | The window cannot exceed 7 days unless the purge changes. |
| Notification | `assignee_id` can be null (unassigned queue). | With no assignee nobody is emailed; the ticket shows up in the queue instead. |
| Access | Portal functions filter by `org_id`. | Reopen must do the same and refuse tickets of other organisations. |
| Existing code | `reopenTicket` in `src/tickets.js` reopens for agents and notifies the assignee. | Call it from the portal; do not write a second transition. |

## Question

| # | Question | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shorten the window to 7 days, or move the purge to 14? | Shorten the window. |

## Requirements

- Reopen button on closed tickets for the agreed window.
- Status becomes open; assignee notified when there is one.
