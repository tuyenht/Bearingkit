# Customer reopen

## Proposal

Add a reopen action to the portal for tickets the customer considers unfinished, and tell the assigned agent.

## Open questions

### Q1. Which tickets is this for?

The glossary says closed tickets are final and never change; the request asks to reopen exactly those. Either the rule goes, or the feature is for resolved tickets.

My recommendation: resolved tickets only. It needs no change to the lifecycle.

### Q2. What happens to the SLA?

A reopened ticket is work again. Does its due time start over?

My recommendation: start it over from the moment of reopening.

## Excluded

- Reopening tickets by email.
- Changing how long a resolved ticket waits before it closes.

## Requirements

- The action is on the ticket page.
- The ticket becomes open and the assigned agent is told.
