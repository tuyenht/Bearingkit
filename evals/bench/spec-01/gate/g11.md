# Portal reopen: specification

## Scope

Customers get a reopen action for closed tickets, available for 14 days. The assigned agent is notified.

## Design

- A new function `reopenFromPortal` in `src/portal.js` performs the status change, and a new route exposes it.
- The purge job keeps running nightly as it does now; this feature does not depend on it.
- The assignee is notified through the email queue. The mail goes to the assignee.
- The customer's organisation is shown in the page header as on the other portal pages.
- SLA handling on reopen is still to be decided.

## Open questions

1. Should the email use a template of its own, and should its subject mention the customer's name? Recommended: its own template; no name in the subject.
2. Should closed tickets show the button in the list view? Recommended: no.

You will also have to decide whether reopened tickets count in the weekly report.

## Exclusions

Anything not mentioned in this document.
