# Spec: customer-initiated reopen

## What was asked, and what the project allows

The request is to let customers reopen closed tickets for 14 days. In this codebase that runs into the status model: `docs/glossary.md` makes closed terminal, and the only status a ticket can come back from is resolved. So the request, taken literally, asks for something the project forbids. I read it as "let the customer take a resolved ticket back to open from the portal", and flag it below so you can overrule me.

## What already exists

- `reopenTicket(store, id, agentId)` in `src/tickets.js` does the transition for agents and emails the assignee. The portal action should go through it instead of duplicating the status change.
- Notifications go through the email queue in `src/notify.js`.

## Constraints found in the code

- Two weeks is longer than a closed ticket keeps its conversation: `purge-closed.js` wipes the messages one week after closing. A ticket reopened on day 10 would come back empty. Under my reading (resolved tickets only) the window is at most the three days before the close job runs.
- Tenant isolation: the action must return "not found" for a ticket whose `org_id` is not the signed-in customer's, like every other portal function.

## Edge cases

- Nobody owns the ticket (`assignee_id` is null): no email is queued; the ticket goes back to the unassigned queue where the next agent picks it up.
- The ticket is already open: the action does nothing and reports success.

## Questions for you

1. Did you mean resolved tickets? Recommendation: yes; keep closed final.
2. Should reopening restart the SLA due time? Recommendation: yes, eight hours from the reopen, because the customer is waiting again.
3. Should a customer have to give a reason? Recommendation: optional text.

## Acceptance criteria

- A customer reopens a resolved ticket of their organisation; status becomes open; the assignee has one queued email.
- A customer of another organisation gets "not found".

## Not part of this work

- Any change to the purge job or to what "closed" means.
- Reopening by replying to an email.
