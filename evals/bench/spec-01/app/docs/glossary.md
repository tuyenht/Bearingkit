# Glossary

The words below mean exactly this in the code, in the back office and in what we tell customers.

**Ticket.** One request from one customer. It belongs to the customer's organisation and never moves to another.

**Status.** A ticket is in one of four statuses:

- **open**: waiting for an agent.
- **pending**: waiting for the customer.
- **resolved**: the agent is done. The customer may still answer, and an agent may reopen the ticket; either puts it back to open.
- **closed**: final. A closed ticket is never changed again. If the customer comes back, they start a new ticket.

**Assignee.** The agent who owns the ticket. A ticket in the unassigned queue has no assignee.

**SLA due time.** When the first answer is due. It is set when the ticket is created.

**Organisation.** The customer's company. A customer only ever sees tickets of their own organisation.
